Players: 1 player
Characters: the main character (A bank robber), the police, and SWAT. 
Clone/Shadows: Every 5 coins collected a new shadow will spawn. This shadow is will mimic the exact movements of the player .5 seconds behing. 
Piggy Banks: Every 12 coins collected a piggy bank will spawn. A police car will chase the player. Every piggy bank collected will add a multiplyier to you overall score. eg. 1 piggy bank = 2x score, 2 piggy bank = 3x score. After collecting 3 coins the police will disappear. The coins can spawn on every platform
Style: The theme should be a bank theme like with money and stuff
On screen: A character running around grabbing coins. The coins will spawn 1 by 1. After 1 is collected, the next one will be collected. Make all characters slower. This game is based on shady bears from shadybears.io
Win/Lose: Collect 1000 coins to win. Get caught by the police to lose. Touch a shadow to lose.
